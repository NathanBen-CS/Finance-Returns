DIFFERENCES FROM PHASE 1'S 61-COMPANY ASSUMPTIONS
   1. Scale: 61 companies became 3,900 in the table and 3,436 here. All 61 Phase 1 tickers are in the table; 58 survive the drop. Lost: AZO, FISV, MU.
   2. founded_year exists in Phase 1 only and is not used.
   3. size_category changed meaning: quartiles of 61 S&P large caps in Phase 1, quartiles of the full census now. All 61 Phase 1 companies are Mega-cap on the new scale; only 14 keep their old tier.
   4. market_cap range: $8,672,975,872 to $4,851,251,544,064 in Phase 1, $32,432 to $5,565,400,481,792 now.
   5. Skew is far heavier: market_cap 3.4 in Phase 1, 22.9 now. log1p brings it to -0.2.
   6. sector is Yahoo's taxonomy now, GICS in Phase 1 (e.g. 'Healthcare' vs 'Health Care'), so labels do not join directly.
   7. trailing_volatility_1y does not exist in Phase 1. After the exclusions it still reaches 3781.1% (IPDN), with skew 23.5. IPDN, DUKR, OPAD show a similar one-way jump and remain in the data.
   8. exchange has 6 codes now (ASE, NCM, NGM, NMS, NYQ, PNK), 2 in Phase 1. PNK is one company (WORX), so its z-scored column is one extreme value.
   9. Blanks: revenue_ttm 1 and employees 1 in Phase 1; 290 and 197 now. Phase 1 has employees for AZO and MU, but those older values were not copied across, so every company follows one rule.
  10. Zero or negative revenue_ttm: 0 in Phase 1, 33 now. Among the companies used, 13 negative values become 0 under the Tier 0 log rule.
  11. Dropping is not random: small companies and Healthcare are dropped most, so the 3,436 lean larger than the full census.
  12. With 22 unit-variance columns, 17 of them one-hot, three components can only reach a low share (33.4%); this reflects the encoding as well as the data.
