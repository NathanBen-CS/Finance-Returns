== Data-quality caveats for modeling ==
  1. Extreme returns kept as reported by Yahoo: 1759 events have |daily_return| or a CAR above 100%
       |daily_return| > 50%:   1374   |CAR[-1,+5]| > 50%:   6245
       |daily_return| > 100%:    371   |CAR[-1,+5]| > 100%:   1570
       |daily_return| > 300%:     82   |CAR[-1,+5]| > 300%:    241
     Some are genuine micro-cap moves; some are likely unadjusted reverse splits (e.g. NFE shows a
     one-day +5,060% close on 2026-08-04, outside any event window). Not removed or winsorized here;
     listed in raw/extreme_return_events.csv. Use winsorizing/robust methods or a Small-cap sensitivity check.
  2. Survivorship bias: the universe is TODAY's SEC ticker list, so companies delisted, acquired or
     bankrupt between 2016 and now are absent. Early years over-represent eventual survivors.
  3. Tiers are measured once, as of 2026-10-08 (market cap at fetch, volatility over the trailing year),
     not at each event date. A 2016 event carries the company's 2026 tier.
  4. sector/sub_industry are Yahoo's (Morningstar-style) taxonomy, not GICS (Phase 1 used GICS).
     sic_code/sic_description are SEC's own classification, included alongside.
  5. filing_timeliness keeps Phase 1's calendar-day buckets; most 'Slightly Late' filings were on time
     under SEC's 4-business-day rule (see timeliness section). Real late filing is rarer than it looks.
  6. 17868 ticker/date pairs have more than one 8-K (37077 events); they share one price reaction, so they are not independent.
  7. ticker = the company's CURRENT ticker; events are pulled by CIK, so ticker changes are handled,
     but price history before a ticker change may be missing on Yahoo (those events are dropped above).
  8. Known residual: FACT (FACT II Acquisition Corp.) is a SPAC that neither screen caught (SEC SIC
     3728, no Yahoo industry). 8-K/A amendments are excluded, as in Phase 1.
