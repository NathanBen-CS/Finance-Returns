FLAGS
  1. The top itemsets follow the most common single items, not strong associations. filing_timeliness=On-time is 88.07% of filings and event_category=Financial/Earnings 44.40%, so the itemsets that pair them with other common items lead. The lift of all 10 rules runs 1.0062 to 1.1110, close to 1 (independence).
  2. Reaction day 0 is the first trading day on or after report_date (the event date), not filing_date. Median trading_date minus filing_date: On-time 0 days, Slightly Late -6, Late -27. So for a Late filing, reaction_category measures the market around the underlying event, about a month before the 8-K was filed, not a reaction to the late filing. Rule A tests whether late filers had different event-day reactions, not whether lateness moved the price.
  3. Rule A rests on few rows: Late is 2,335 of 411,440 filings (0.57%), and 854 of them are Financial/Earnings; the smallest cell behind a Financial/Earnings lift is 107 rows. Late filings by event_category: Financial/Earnings 854, Other 524, Management/Governance 481, M&A/Contracts 409, Legal/Regulatory 49, Capital/Financing 17, Security Incident 1.
  4. Rule A controls for event_category (by fixing it) and, in the matched columns, for size_category x volatility_tier. It does not control for the company itself; companies that file late repeatedly count once per filing.
  5. volatility_tier is circular as a predictor of reaction size: it is quartiles of trailing_volatility_1y, a single October 2026 value computed partly from the same returns the reactions are measured on. Itemsets and rules pairing volatility_tier with a Strong reaction partly restate that.
  6. size_category, volatility_tier and sector are October 2026 snapshots attached to filings back to 2016-01-04. size_category is quartiles of this dataset's companies (Mega-cap starts at $5.8B here).
  7. Rows are not independent: 38,102 (9.26%) share a company and trading day with another filing and therefore share its CAR and reaction_category. Support counts each of them; chi-square p-values treat them as independent, so read them as optimistic.
